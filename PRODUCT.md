# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Business operators, DevOps engineers, government IT teams, field workers, and grant administrators. They manage ticket-based workflows across organizations and need a platform that handles routing, automation, and AI-assisted triage without the usual enterprise software friction.

## Product Purpose

Ticket Masala is an intelligent workflow orchestration platform. The marketing site exists to explain what it does, show off its multi-tenant architecture through live demos, and convert visitors into users or contributors (it's open source). Success means visitors understand the product's value in under 30 seconds and click through to docs or a demo.

## Brand Personality

Playful, approachable, clever. The voice should feel like a sharp engineer who explains things clearly without dumbing them down. Confidence comes from competence, not from corporate posturing. Humor is welcome when it serves clarity.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS landing pages**: hero-metric-card-grid-testimonial-CTA template with interchangeable copy. The "Stop X. Start Y." pattern. Cream/beige backgrounds with gradient accents.
- **Dated enterprise software sites**: corporate blue palette, stock photography, buzzword-heavy copy ("leverage", "seamless", "next-generation"), cluttered navigation.
- **Over-designed AI portfolios**: glassmorphism, gradient text, floating orbs, neon accents, hand-drawn SVG illustrations, decorative blurs used as texture.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the product, don't describe it.** Live demos and real architecture screenshots do more than paragraphs of features. The "One Core. Infinite Skins." section is the strongest proof point on the site.
2. **Earn the complexity.** Workflow orchestration is genuinely complex. Don't hide that, but make each layer of complexity feel manageable. Clarity over simplicity.
3. **Playful doesn't mean unprofessional.** The tone can be light and clever while the information stays precise. A good reference is how Stripe or Vercel balance personality with technical rigor.
4. **Multi-tenant is the story.** The 5-theme architecture is the product's most distinctive feature. Every design decision should reinforce that this is one platform with many faces.
5. **Open source pride.** The "100% Open Source" badge isn't decoration. It shapes the voice (transparent, community-oriented) and the visual language (no dark patterns, no paywall gates).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Skip links, ARIA labels, and focus states are already in place. Maintain contrast ratios (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text), keyboard navigation for all interactive elements, and screen reader support. Reduced motion media queries for scroll animations. The 5-theme system must maintain accessible contrast in every theme, not just the default.
